Every account register and **All Transactions** has a search box above the table. Type what you're looking for — `groceries last month`, `outflows over 100`, `uncleared` — and Budgero turns the words it recognises into filters. Each recognised filter shows up as a chip under the box; click its **×** to drop it. Whatever is left over is searched as plain text.

All filters combine: `Dining out last month over 30` shows Dining out transactions from last month above 30.

The filter words are English keywords, whatever language the app is in.

A date word also moves the register's date range at the top of the page, so the table loads the right period. Weeks start on the day set in **Settings → Budget Settings → First day of the week**.

## Money in, money out, transfers

- `inflows`, `inflow`, or `income` — money coming in (transfers excluded).
- `outflows`, `outflow`, `expenses`, `expense`, or `spending` — money going out (transfers excluded).
- `transfers` or `transfer` — only transfers between your accounts.

## Cleared status

- `cleared` — cleared transactions, including reconciled ones.
- `uncleared` — transactions you haven't cleared yet.
- `reconciled` — only reconciled transactions.

You can also write them as `is:cleared`, `is:uncleared`, and `is:reconciled`. Scheduled recurring rows (the projected ones) never match a status.

## Amounts

Search by amount with a comparison word or symbol in front of the number:

  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>You type</th>
      <th>Finds amounts</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td><code>over 50</code>, <code>above 50</code>, <code>&gt;50</code></td><td>Greater than 50</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>under 20</code>, <code>below 20</code>, <code>&lt;20</code></td><td>Less than 20</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>exactly 42.50</code>, <code>equal 42.50</code>, <code>=42.50</code></td><td>42.50 (to the cent)</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

- **Ranges:** combine both ends — `over 20 under 50`.
- **Direction doesn't matter:** an amount filter compares the size of the transaction, whether it's money in or out. Add `outflows` or `inflows` to narrow it down.
- **Currency:** amounts are compared in the currency the register shows (account or budget currency, from the toggle above the table). A leading `$`, `€`, or `£` is allowed: `over €100`.
- **Number format:** use a dot for decimals and no thousands separators — `=1250.50`, not `=1.250,50` or `=1,250.50`.
- **A number on its own is text,** not an amount: `42.50` looks for "42.50" in memos and payees. Use `=42.50` to find the amount.

## Categories and labels

- Type a **category name** to show only that category. Names with spaces go in quotes: `"Dining out"`. While you type, Budgero suggests matching categories; pick one to add it. Add several categories to see any of them.
- Use `label:` for **labels**: `label:Vacation`, or `label:"Work trip"` for names with spaces.

## Plain text

Anything the search doesn't recognise is matched against the **memo, payee, category, label, and account** of each transaction, ignoring upper and lower case. `pharmacy` finds "City Pharmacy" as a payee or a memo.

## Quick filters

Next to the search, the **Uncleared** and **Uncategorized** buttons show how many such transactions there are and filter to them in one click. On an account page, <Keys>Shift+C</Keys> and <Keys>Shift+U</Keys> toggle them from the keyboard — see [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts).
